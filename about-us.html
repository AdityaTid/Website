Our Business

Tista Ventures is a fast growing trading & services company delivering best-in-class construction products and services for fast growing infrastructure. We are construction material supplier for a range of products starting from fasteners, mechanical anchors etc.

Our clients

The clients of the company are spread through the length and breadth of India. Presently, we are dealing with products to cater to various industries like:

  • Construction Industry
  • Solar Industry
  • Paint and Coatings Industry
  • Health Industry and FMCG Industry

Our Vission

Tista Ventures is committed to being the construction material supply industry's most trusted and relied-upon partner, providing best-in-class products, exceptional expertise and a passion for quality and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To work progressively toward redefining distribution process by bringing in technology products and solutions, fulfilling on-going market demand and by adding value to both vendor and reseller through timely availability of the goods , product knowledge and value adding to profitable working relationship.

To work effectively towards creating demand for products and passage to market for vendor and by generation of beneficial opportunities for our reseller partner by creation of effective rewards programme, efficient supply chain and knowledge by people training.

As a global industry leader we focus on long-term business success through the distribution of premium construction products and services, while always embracing our company objectives to:

  • Develop long-term partnerships with our customers and suppliers.
  • Consistently meet our customers' expectations with our service and product quality.
  • Work with honesty, integrity and respect at all times.
  • Continuously improve our best practices to maximize environmental, social and economic sustainability.
  • Embrace change and encourage innovation.
  • Offer our employees a challenging and rewarding workplace that inspires loyalty and success.
  • Seek excellence in everything we do.
  • Respect the environment and communities within which we operate.
