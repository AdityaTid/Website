About Tista Ventures LLP

Building Stronger Connections. Delivering Trusted Solutions.

Exclusive Channel Partner for KLIMAS & SIKA | Mumbai Region

Tista Ventures LLP is a professionally managed trading and distribution company committed to providing premium-quality construction products to contractors, façade consultants, structural engineers, architects, project management consultants, builders, and industrial clients.

Our philosophy is simple: deliver genuine products, expert technical guidance, timely deliveries, and exceptional customer service.

With a customer-centric approach and strong relationships across the construction ecosystem, we ensure every client receives solutions tailored to their project requirements.

Our Brands

KLIMAS — Global Fastening Solutions

KLIMAS is one of Europe's leading manufacturers of professional fastening systems designed for modern construction.

Our KLIMAS product range includes:

  • Mechanical Anchor Fasteners
  • Chemical Anchoring Systems
  • Concrete Fasteners
  • Frame Fixings
  • Roofing Fasteners
  • Wood Construction Fasteners
  • Drywall Fixings
  • Heavy Duty Structural Anchors
  • Installation Accessories

SIKA — Specialty Sealants & Silicones

Speciality sealants & silicones for façade, fenestration and insulation. SIKA is a global leader in specialty chemicals for construction.

We offer a comprehensive range of SIKA solutions including:

  • Structural Sealants
  • Weather Seal Silicones
  • Fire Rated Sealants
  • PU Sealants
  • Hybrid Sealants
  • Waterproofing Solutions

Suitable for: Commercial Buildings, Airports, Metro Projects, Hospitals, Hotels, Residential Developments, Industrial Facilities, Façade Engineering.
