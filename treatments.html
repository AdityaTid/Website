Treatments

Classical procedures performed at the centre. Your plan is chosen after consultation with Dr. Mayuri Golap-Deshmukh and only when a therapy is appropriate for you.

Shirodhara

A steady stream of warm medicated oil on the forehead to settle the mind and support sleep and stress balance, as part of a supervised plan.

Nasya

Medicated oils or powders given through the nasal route for head, neck, and clarity-related care when indicated.

Vamana

Therapeutic emesis used in classical Panchakarma for Kapha-related conditions, only after preparation and strict medical supervision.

Virechana

Therapeutic purgation to clear excess Pitta and support digestion and liver–gut balance, carried out only after oleation and preparation under supervision.

Janu basti

Warm medicated oil held over the knee region in a ring of dough to nourish the joint and support knee comfort and mobility.

Kati basti

Oil pooling over the lower back for lumbar and muscular ease, often used alongside broader spine and Vata-care protocols.

Manya basti

Oil retained over the neck region to ease stiffness, tension, and cervical discomfort as part of your prescribed course.

Rakta mokshana

Controlled bloodletting by classical method when blood tissue (Rakta) imbalance is identified and the procedure is judged suitable for you.
