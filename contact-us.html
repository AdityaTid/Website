Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our products and services

What's your shipping policy?

We offer free standard shipping on orders over $100. Most orders ship within 24 hours and arrive within 2-3 business days. Express shipping options are available.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes! We provide significant discounts for bulk orders. Contact our sales team for custom quotes on large quantity purchases for contractors and businesses.

Are your products covered by warranty?

All our products come with a minimum 1-year warranty. Many premium items have extended warranties up to 5 years. Specific warranty details are provided with each product.

Can I return products if I'm not satisfied?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. Unused products in original packaging can be returned for a full refund. Some restrictions apply to opened adhesives and chemicals.
